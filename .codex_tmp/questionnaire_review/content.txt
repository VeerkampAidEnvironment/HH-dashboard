      R1: Pass || 2 points

[006] P (Normal): Package score = points earned ÷ points available × 100.
[008] P (Heading 2): Instructions
[009] P (List Paragraph): Walking interview: Section B is conducted as a walking interview - the CBF asks questions while observing the plot directly. Observation is the control: where a question can be answered by what the farmer says or by what the CBF sees, the physical observation takes precedence.
[010] P (List Paragraph): Critical questions act as a switch. If triggered, the whole package fails outright (0%), regardless of the point total. Example: 14/16 points in Soil Cover & Fertility = 88% only if the package’s critical question(s) passed. If not, the result is failed, even though the points suggest a strong score.
